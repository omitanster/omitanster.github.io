At Moomin Tea, we believe great tea creates the perfect moment to slow down. Our shop is designed as a warm, modern space where friends gather, conversations flow, and every sip feels intentional. Inspired by contemporary tea houses and soft Nordic color palettes, Moomin Tea blends natural wood textures, calming teal and blush tones, and bright open interiors to create a place where guests can truly relax.

Our menu celebrates thoughtfully crafted drinks that balance flavor, freshness, and creativity. From vibrant Guava Jasmine tea to rich Matcha Einspanner, smooth Matcha Lattes, and comforting Banana Bread Latte coffee, every drink is made with carefully sourced ingredients and brewed with precision. Whether you're craving something refreshing, creamy, or bold, our menu is designed to offer a drink for every mood.

Moomin Tea is more than a place to grab a drink — it’s a space to chat, relax, and sip. Our long tables, cozy seating, and welcoming atmosphere invite you to stay a little longer. Students meet here to study, friends gather for conversation, and tea lovers discover new favorites. Every detail of the shop is crafted to encourage connection and calm.

We’re proud to be a modern tea house where creativity meets comfort. At Moomin Tea, every cup is an invitation to pause, enjoy the moment, and share something special with the people around you.
